About Me

Chloe Gorgue is a student at Arizona State University studying Media Arts and Sciences with a focus on games and interactive media, and minoring in animation. This website is a collection of my work and projects that I have completed during my time at ASU.

Background

I am a passionate student with a strong interest in game development and interactive media. My academic journey at Arizona State University has provided me with a solid foundation in both the technical and creative aspects of media arts.

Skills

I have developed a diverse skill set that includes programming, animation, and design. My coursework and projects have allowed me to gain hands-on experience in creating engaging and interactive media experiences.

Interests

Beyond my academic pursuits, I have a keen interest in exploring new technologies and trends in the media arts field. I enjoy collaborating with others on creative projects and continuously seek opportunities to expand my knowledge and skills.

Contact
